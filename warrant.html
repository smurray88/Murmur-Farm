Race record

Stakes wins and graded placings

A homebred for Twin Creeks Farm, Warrant won or placed in 11 stakes. His wins include the Oklahoma Derby (G3), the Texas Derby and the Isaac Murphy Marathon Stakes.

His best figure came in the Santa Anita Handicap (G1), where he ran a 104 Beyer and missed by only a head. He also placed in the West Virginia Derby (G3), Louisiana Stakes (G3), Cornhusker Handicap (G3) and Brooklyn Stakes (G2).

At stud

Warrant entered stud in Pennsylvania in 2024 and covered a reported state-leading 49 mares in his first season. He moved to Murmur Farm that fall and stood his first Maryland season in 2025.

“He's been at the farm maybe three weeks so far and he settled in fine.”

Mrs. Audrey Murray, to The Racing Biz, November 2024
