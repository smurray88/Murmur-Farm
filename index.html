Our Legacy

The little farm that stood a Derby sire

Allen and Audrey Murray started with an $800 broodmare in 1959. In 2002, Our Emblem, the stallion they had bought a year earlier, sired Kentucky Derby and Preakness winner War Emblem.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Murmur Farm take outside mares?

Yes. Murmur Farm boards, foals and lays up outside mares, not only mares booked to our stallions. Permanent and seasonal boarding are both available.

What are your boarding, foaling and layup rates?

Please call the farm at (410) 836-3491 for current rates.

What are the 2026 stud fees for Blofeld and Warrant?

Blofeld stands for $5,000 live foal and Warrant for $4,500 live foal. Both fees are payable when the foal stands and nurses.

Are Blofeld and Warrant nominated to the Maryland Million and the Breeders' Cup?

Yes. Both stallions are nominated to the Maryland Million and the Breeders' Cup, and stallion compatibility analysis is available for each.

Who watches the mares during foaling season?

A night watchman checks on the mares throughout the night during foaling season.

Can I visit to see the stallions?

Yes. Stallion showings are available by appointment. Call (410) 836-3491 to arrange a visit.

Where is Murmur Farm, and how far is it from the tracks and sales?

Murmur Farm is at 1334 Stafford Road, Darlington, Maryland 21034, in northern Harford County. By road it is about 25 miles to Fair Hill Training Center, about 35 miles to Delaware Park, about 35 miles to the Maryland State Fairgrounds in Timonium, about 40 miles to Pimlico and about 65 miles to Laurel Park.

How long has Murmur Farm been in business?

Allen and Audrey Murray founded Murmur Farm in 1961. The farm stood Our Emblem, sire of 2002 Kentucky Derby and Preakness winner War Emblem.

Do you have any horses for sale?

Not at this time. Call the farm to ask about upcoming offerings.

Booking a mare for 2026?

Call Mrs. Audrey Murray to talk through a mating or arrange a visit.
